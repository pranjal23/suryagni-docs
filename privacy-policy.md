Suryagni Privacy Policy

Data Collection:
To generate precise astrological charts and PDF reports, Suryagni requires users to input specific personal information, including their Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Location of Birth (City/Coordinates).

Data Processing and Storage:
Suryagni is a local desktop application. All astrological calculations and PDF generation are performed locally on the user's device.

Data Sharing:
Suryagni does not transmit, upload, or share your personal birth data with any external servers, third-party databases, or analytics services. Your personal information remains strictly on your device.
